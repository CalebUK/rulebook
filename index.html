Opening the rulebook…

        How to play

        1. You get one rule, taken from a real sport's official rulebook. Guess the sport.
        2. Every wrong guess reveals another rule, each one easier than the last.
        3. You have five guesses. A close guess is in the same family as the answer, like ice hockey when the answer is field hockey.

        Words in brackets have been swapped out because they'd give the game away. A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight.
